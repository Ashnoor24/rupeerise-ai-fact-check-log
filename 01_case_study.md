# Step 1: The RupeeRise Case Study

**RupeeRise** is a fictional digital lending app in Bengaluru that gives small personal loans to young salaried workers. Its founder is meeting investors next week and asked an AI tool to write a one-page brief on India's digital lending and UPI market.

The founder asks you, a Business Analyst: *"The brief reads really well. Can I send it to investors as it is?"* Your job is to find out, claim by claim.

## Approved Official Sources for Verification

| Data Domain | Only Accepted Proof Source |
| :--- | :--- |
| **UPI and payments data** | NPCI website (`npci.org.in`) |
| **Lending rules and circulars** | RBI website (`rbi.org.in`) |
| **Government announcements** | PIB (`pib.gov.in`) |
| **Company figures** | The company's own annual report or official press release |
| **Not good enough** | Blogs, social media, news without a source, or another AI tool's answer |
